Places

We gathered a list with spaces you can check out

LGBT+ Danmark

Vestergade 18E, 2nd floor, 1456 Copenhagen K

Denmark's national LGBTQ+ organization offering advocacy, counseling, and community events.

BLUS LGBT+ Student Organization

Studenterhuset, Købmagergade 52, 1150 Copenhagen K

A student-run LGBTQ+ organization that creates social and cultural events for queer students.

Bøssehuset

A historic queer cultural house in Christiania hosting theater, performances, exhibitions, and community events.

Warehouse9

A queer-led art and performance venue known for experimental shows, exhibitions, and festivals.

The Pond

A queer bookstore and café created as a safe and welcoming community space for LGBTQIA+ people.

Never Mind Night Club

One of Copenhagen’s largest LGBTQ+ nightclubs with a strong focus on inclusivity and safe nightlife.

Trixie

A queer-friendly bar and club known for electronic music and an inclusive crowd.

Kvindehuset

A feminist cultural house that frequently hosts queer, lesbian, and intersectional events.

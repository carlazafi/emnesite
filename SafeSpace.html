Safer spaces

A safe space is an environment where people—especially those from marginalized communities such as LGBTQ+ individuals—can express themselves freely without fear of discrimination, harassment, or violence. In a safe space, respect, acceptance, and inclusion are actively promoted, and harmful behavior such as bullying or hate speech is not tolerated. These spaces can exist in schools, workplaces, community centers, or online platforms and aim to support people’s dignity, identity, and well-being. Source: Safe Space Alliance – definition of a safe space for LGBTQ+ communities.
